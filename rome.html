Rome

Sat Sep 26 – Wed Sep 30

Day by dayGiorno per giorno
  • Sat · Sep 26
    Arrive. Taxi or public transport from the airport to the Airbnb? Get settled, aperitivo at a rooftop bar, dinner, then a nighttime walk or golf-cart tour near the Trevi Fountain. (All optional — we might be too tired.)
  • Sun · Sep 27
    Morning: the Pantheon (€10 skip-the-line) and the piazzas of the Centro Storico, with lunch in Monti. Later: Roman Forum & Palatine Hill on the Colosseum ticket, then the guided Colosseum tour at 4:45 PM with Agostino.
  • Mon · Sep 28
    Morning at the Vatican (skip-the-line pass). Free afternoon and evening. (Many restaurants close on Mondays, so a good day for McDonald's and the more touristy spots.)
  • Tue · Sep 29
    Stay in Rome, or a day trip? Options: a bike ride on the Appian Way, a beach, or a more ambitious trip to Abruzzo for the mountains. My favorite idea — a short train to the ancient town of Tivoli and a 1:00 PM lunch reservation at Ristorante Sibilla to sit outside with beautiful views beside ancient ruins.
  • Wed · Sep 30
    Earliest breakfast, then the high-speed train to Venice (~3.5 hrs).
