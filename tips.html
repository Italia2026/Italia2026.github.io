General Tips & Cultural Advice

The stuff that makes the trip smoother.

Money & payments

Cards work almost everywhere; carry some euros for buses, small cafés and tips. Tell your bank you're travelling.

Getting around

High-speed trains between cities (book ahead for the best fares), local buses on the coast, and rental cars in Tuscany.

Etiquette

A quick 'buongiorno' goes a long way. Cover shoulders and knees in churches. Dinner runs late — 8 PM is normal.

Safety & essentials

Watch for pickpockets around big sights and stations. Save the EU emergency number: 112.
