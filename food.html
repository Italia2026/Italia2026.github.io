How dining works

Coffee standing at the bar is cheap and quick. Lunch ~1 PM, dinner from 8 PM. A small cover charge (coperto) is normal.

What to try where

Cacio e pepe in Rome, cicchetti in Venice, bistecca in Tuscany, seafood and lemons on the Amalfi Coast.
