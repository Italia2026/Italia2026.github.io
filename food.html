Food & Dining

Eat well, order with confidence, and handle the bill.

How dining works

Lunch runs about 1–2:30, dinner opens at 730 or 8. A small cover charge (coperto) per person is normal and not a scam — it is traditionally much less than a tip, which isn't expected the way it is back home. Tap water isn't usually served — you order a bottle, naturale (still) or frizzante (sparkling). A a cappuccino after lunch is harmless and will be accommodated, but is traditionally never requested — Italians keep milky coffee to the morning. Apart from tourist hotspots, it is considered rude for waiters to bring you a check since long meals are meant to be enjoyed with family and friends. The check is requested by the customers when they are ready.
